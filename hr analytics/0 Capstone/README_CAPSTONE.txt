PRIMARY KEY
Employee_ID connects the employee-level datasets.
Application_ID and Training_ID are transaction-level keys.

SUGGESTED CAPSTONE QUESTIONS
- What is current and historical headcount/FTE by department, location and grade?
- Which workforce segments have the highest attrition?
- What drives voluntary and regrettable attrition?
- Which recruitment sources deliver faster hiring, better conversion and stronger employee outcomes?
- Where are absenteeism and overtime concentrated?
- Are compensation, performance and engagement patterns related to attrition?
- What is the promotion/high-potential/succession pipeline?
- Does training participation relate to performance improvement?
- Which departments show workforce-capacity risks?
- Build an executive People Analytics dashboard and an attrition prediction model.
